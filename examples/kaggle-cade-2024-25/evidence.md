## 样本限制与缺失

- EPM: Estimate type/date/window not verified; 2025 actual unavailable
- shot_coordinates: Coordinate system and full shot coverage not verified
- team_on_off: Mislabelled opponent-vs-team fields; use player_onoff only
- Playoffs: Season-table player identities incomplete; adapter is regular-season-only
- Player sample is full-season, not a team stint. TEAM_ID may label a latest team; do not assume all minutes were played for that team or that team context is a causal effect.
