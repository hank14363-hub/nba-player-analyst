## 样本限制与缺失

- 高阶数据默认采集状态：partial；各模型结果见 impact_collection_audit.json。
- 同角色比较已核验；正文应说明实际值、中位数和门槛敏感性，百分位不是能力评分。
- Impact LEBRON: Impact minute coverage mismatch
- Impact EPM: access_restricted: Historical/provider access restricted; no workaround attempted
- Player sample is full-season, not a team stint. TEAM_ID may label a latest team; do not assume all minutes were played for that team or that team context is a causal effect.
- P_SHOT: missing coordinates 2; missing zone labels 2. Location charts use known-coordinate events; all events remain in season and zone totals.
- DEFENSIVE_ABILITY_SCORE: No validated individual defensive model; never derive this from steals, blocks, or on-court defensive rating.
