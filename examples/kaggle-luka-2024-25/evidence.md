## 样本限制与缺失

- Kaggle historical snapshot; BR rate definitions/precision differ from NBA Stats. Single-player import is not a complete league cohort.
- EPM: Estimate type/date/window not verified; 2025 actual unavailable
- shot_coordinates: Coordinate system and full shot coverage not verified
- team_on_off: Mislabelled opponent-vs-team fields; use player_onoff only
- Playoffs: Season-table player identities incomplete; adapter is regular-season-only
- player_onoff: Needs one actual sample team, matching rows and an independent official team-minute reference; never use On+Off as its own baseline
- Target team context unavailable. Tactical fit must be conditional; no unverified roster/coach claims.
- Player sample is full-season, not a team stint. TEAM_ID may label a latest team; do not assume all minutes were played for that team or that team context is a causal effect.
- LEAGUE_TS_PCT: Full, unfiltered league team totals unavailable. Never average player TS%.
- RTS_PP: Full, unfiltered league team totals unavailable. Never average player TS%.
- ONCOURT_OFF_RATING: player_advanced.OFF_RATING missing or unverified; not inferred from box-score totals.
- ONCOURT_DEF_RATING: player_advanced.DEF_RATING missing or unverified; not inferred from box-score totals.
- ONCOURT_NET_RATING: player_advanced.NET_RATING missing or unverified; not inferred from box-score totals.
- PCT_AST_FGM: player_scoring.PCT_AST_FGM missing or unverified; not inferred from box-score totals.
- PCT_UAST_FGM: player_scoring.PCT_UAST_FGM missing or unverified; not inferred from box-score totals.
- TEAM_OFF_RATING: Target team advanced data missing.
- TEAM_DEF_RATING: Target team advanced data missing.
- TEAM_NET_RATING: Target team advanced data missing.
- TEAM_PACE: Target team advanced data missing.
- TEAM_AST_PCT: Target team advanced data missing.
- TEAM_FG3A_RATE: Team counts missing or zero FGA.
- ON_OFF_DIFFERENTIAL: Matching reliable On and Off rows are unavailable or rejected. On-court or overall team ratings cannot substitute for Off.
- DEFENSIVE_ABILITY_SCORE: No validated individual defensive model; never derive this from steals, blocks, or on-court defensive rating.
